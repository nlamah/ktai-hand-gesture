\section{Standalone G/H Vertical-Axis Rotation}\label{app:gh-y180}
\subsection{Question and Separation from Other Experiments}
Does adding a $180^\circ$ camera-$y$ turn to G/H fitting examples improve the 26-letter SVM? Neither arm uses mirror augmentation, camera calibration or a routing rule. This differs from RGB mirroring (Appendix~L) and the earlier multi-angle recipe (Appendix~J). Requested after earlier development, it is exploratory, not an untouched confirmatory study or an angle search.

Observed palm-facing dataset examples and back-facing webcam poses motivated the turn; these views are not verified across all source images. The transformation uses the normalized image-landmark geometry of Shin's directions:
\[
R_y(180^\circ)=\begin{bmatrix}-1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&-1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad d'_{ij}=R_y(180^\circ)d_{ij}.
\]
This equals a wrist-centred landmark turn, $p'_i=p_0+R_y(180^\circ)(p_i-p_0)$, since translation cancels in pair directions. Unlike horizontal reflection, both $x$ and estimated $z$ change; the determinant is $+1$. Landmark identities, letter labels, 190 normalized distances and 15 intrinsic world-joint measurements stay fixed. No new RGB view or hidden fingers are reconstructed.

\subsection{Matched Fitting and Evaluation}
Both arms use the historical 60,251 accepted originals, including 2,430 G and 2,371 H. A freshly fitted reference exactly reproduces all historical held-out predictions. Each G/H fitting source retains its original and adds one turn, weighted 0.5 each; other sources retain weight one. The full fit has 65,052 rows from 60,251 sources. Both are ordinary 835-input, 26-class RBF SVMs with $C=10$, sharing original-fit imputation/scaling, resolved gamma and balanced class priors.

The same five filename-block folds are retained; original/turned siblings never cross partitions. Each source receives one held-out prediction per arm on its unchanged original. Blocks are not verified people. Scores condition on successful historical detection (77.24\% of 78,000 sources), not an end-to-end or independent back-view test.

\subsection{Limits and Reproducibility}
This isolates G/H turns on the original-data recognizer, not interactions with mirroring, light or calibration. Rotating normalized descriptors cannot simulate real visibility, perspective or landmark errors. Source-view scores and selected error recovery do not establish back-view robustness. Feature design and augmentation remain distinct research questions.

\texttt{results/rotation\_GH\_y180\_01/} stores the frozen protocol, hashes, all predictions, fold/letter scores, paired changes, confusions and camera replay. Verification recomputes metrics, checks disjoint groups, original-only preprocessing and source weights, replays fold models and reconciles the historical reference. \texttt{report\_gh\_rotation\_standalone.py} generates the paper numbers; \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} gives commands. These checks support computational consistency, not independent linguistic or anatomical validation.
